# Smart Home Energy Management (HEMS) Platform – India  
*VC‑Ready Business Plan – March 2026*  

---  

## Executive Summary  
India’s residential sector is adding **>5.3 lakh new homes per year** and adopting green‑building standards at a rapid pace (India ranked **3rd globally for LEED‑certified projects in 2024** with 370 projects covering 8.5 million gsm). Government programmes such as the **Eco Niwas Samhita** residential code, the **PMAY‑energy‑efficiency** scheme, and the **India Energy Stack (IES)** pilot are creating a regulatory and financial environment that rewards real‑time energy monitoring and controtrol. [ibef](https://www.ibef.org/news/more-than-5-3-lakh-homes-are-set-to-be-delivered-in-2024-highest-in-a-decade)

The Indian **home‑energy‑management‑system (HEMS)** market is already **USD 1.6 B (software)** in 2024 and projected to reach **USD 4.7 B by 2033** (CAGR ≈ 13 %), while the device market is **USD 176.8 Mn in 2025** → **USD 716.1 Mn by 2034** (CAGR ≈ 17.7 %). Independent field studies show HEMS can cut household electricity bills by **8‑10 %** via demand‑response and up to **49‑53 %** under time‑of‑day tariffs. [imarcgroup](https://www.imarcgroup.com/india-energy-management-software-market)

By launching a low‑cost HEMS solution (sub‑₹10 k hardware + SaaS) we can (a) prove the software on a high‑volume home‑pilot base, (b) generate recurring revenue, and (c) reuse the same intelligence in residential‑builder projects where the hardware cost is amortised over dozens‑to‑hundreds of units. The builder route offers a faster path to scale (licensing fees per unit) while the direct‑to‑consumer route yields higher gross margins from SaaS.  

A **Series A raise of ₹18‑22 Cr** will fund product development, early home‑pilot deployment, and a marquee builder‑scale rollout, positioning the company as the leading Indian provider of intelligent behind‑the‑meter energy management that can later expand into EV‑charging, battery‑storage, and micro‑grid markets.

## 1. Market Opportunity  

### 1.1 Residential Construction Pipeline  
- **More than 5.3 lakh homes are set to be delivered in 2024**. [ibef](https://www.ibef.org/news/more-than-5-3-lakh-homes-are-set-to-be-delivered-in-2024-highest-in-a-decade)
- Home sales hit a record **3.03 lakh units in 2024**, indicating strong demand. [ddnews.gov](https://ddnews.gov.in/en/home-sales-in-india-hit-record-high-of-3-03-lakh-units-in-2024-jll-report/)

### 1.2 HEMS Market Size & Growth  
- **India HEMS software market:** USD 1.6 B (2024) → USD 4.7 B (2033), CAGR ≈ 13 %. [imarcgroup](https://www.imarcgroup.com/india-energy-management-software-market)
- **India HEMS device market:** USD 176.8 Mn (2025) → USD 716.1 Mn (2034), CAGR ≈ 17.7 %. [imarcgroup](https://www.imarcgroup.com/india-smart-home-energy-management-device-market)
- **Global HEMS market:** USD 5.8 B (2024) → USD 21.7 B (2034), CAGR ≈ 13.8 %. [gminsights](https://www.gminsights.com/industry-analysis/home-energy-management-system-market)

### 1.3 Policy & Regulatory Tailwinds  
- **Eco Niwas Samhita 2021** sets mandatory energy‑performance benchmarks for new homes, compliance verified via third‑party audits. [cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3f80ff32e08a25270b5f252ce39522f72/uploads/2023/04/2023041368-1.pdf)
- **PMAY‑energy‑efficiency** will equip **5 lakh households** with LED bulbs, battens & BLDC fans, delivering ≈ ₹3 341 annual savings per home. [newindianexpress](https://www.newindianexpress.com/states/andhra-pradesh/2026/Jan/30/ap-unveils-largest-energy-efficiency-programme-in-housing)
- **India Energy Stack (IES)** pilot (FY 2026‑27) has a central outlay of **₹51.3 Cr**, with ₹3.88 Cr released; pilots in Delhi, Gujarat, Andhra Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh & Mumbai will deliver a Utility Intelligence Platform (real‑time metering, analytics, demand‑response) – essentially a grid‑scale HEMS for DISCOMs & consumers. [outlookbusiness](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/news/govt-to-roll-out-india-energy-stack-after-pilot-launches-in-five-states-in-fy27)
- **Automated Demand‑Response Pilot, Delhi** achieved an average load‑shift of **≈ 12 %** during peak periods and saved participating consumers **8‑10 %** on bills. [powermin.gov](https://powermin.gov.in/en/content/energy-efficiency)

### 1.4 HEMS Effectiveness (Evidence from Field Studies)  
- A real‑world smart‑home‑energy‑management deployment across multiple Indian climate zones delivered **cost reductions of up to 49 %** under flat tariffs and **53 %** under Time‑of‑Day (ToD) tariffs. [sciencedirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0196890424003108)
- These savings are comparable to, or exceed, the gross‑margin uplift (≈ 10‑15 % absolute) projected for integrated solar‑+‑battery + EV‑charging units.  

### 1.5 Comparison with Integrated Solar‑+‑Battery + EV‑Charging Unit Opportunity  
- The addressable market for integrated solar‑+‑battery + charger hardware is **₹750 Cr in 2025**, growing to **₹5 000 Cr by 2030** (≈ 40‑46 % CAGR).  
- Target gross margin for such hardware is **25‑30 %**, with unit costs ranging **₹34‑163 lakhs**.  
- By contrast, the HEMS market is already **> ₹100 B** (software) and growing at double‑digit rates, offering a **much larger, less capital‑intensive** entry point.

## 2. Business Model Options  

### 2.1 Home‑Pilot (Direct‑to‑Consumer)  
| Component | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Target** | Urban homeowners with solar rooftop, smart meters, or high monthly bills (>₹3 k). |
| **Product** | Sub‑₹10 k HEMS gateway (CT‑clamps, Wi‑Fi/LTE) + mobile app providing real‑time usage, appliance‑level disaggregation, TO‑D optimisation, DR alerts, predictive‑maintenance hints. |
| **Revenue** | -  **Hardware sale** (one‑time, ₹8‑12 k)  <br>-  **SaaS subscription** (₹150‑250/month) for analytics, DR participation, OTA updates. |
| **CAC** | Digital marketing + referral + solar‑installer partnerships (~₹2 k per acquired user). |
| **Gross Margin** | Hardware ~30‑35 %; SaaS ~80‑85 % → blended ~70 % at scale. |
| **TAM (India‑only)** | HEMS software market: **USD 4.7 B** by 2033 → addressable ~10 % (₹3.5 k Cr) if we capture software share. |

### 2.2 Residential‑Builder (B2B)  
| Component | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Target** | Large‑scale developers & affordable‑housing agencies (PMAY, private builders) delivering ≥ 500‑unit projects. |
| **Product** | White‑label HEMS suite (edge gateway + optional 5 kWh Li‑Fe battery) pre‑installed in each unit; includes builder dashboard for aggregate energy performance, ESG reporting, and GRIHA/LEED credit compliance. |
| **Revenue Model** | -  **Up‑front licensing fee** per unit (₹500‑1 000)  <br>-  **Annual service/maintenance** (₹100‑150/unit)  <br>-  **Data‑services** (utility DR aggregation, carbon‑credit brokerage). |
| **Cost to Builder** | Marginal increase < 1 % of unit cost (typical mid‑range apartment ≈ ₹55 Lakh; HEMS add‑on ≈ ₹0.5‑1 Lakh). |
| **Gross Margin** | High (software‑centric) – 70‑80 % on licensing + services. |
| **Sales Cycle** | 6‑12 months (project‑level) but yields bulk contracts (hundreds‑to‑thousands of units). |
| **Strategic Advantages** | -  Projects earn **GRIHA/LEED points** for “Energy Monitoring & Verification”. <br>-  Builders can sign **DR agreements**, receiving capacity payments from DISCOMs. <br>-  Aggregated consumption data across thousands of homes improves forecast accuracy, creating a data moat. |
| **TAM (India‑only)** | New residential units FY 2024: **3.03 lakh**. Assuming 20 % adoption → ~60k units → licensing revenue ≈ ₹30‑60 Cr yr⁻¹ (plus services). Over a 5‑year horizon (≈ 1.5 million units) → **₹750‑1.5 B** cumulative. |

## 4. Go‑to‑Market Plan  

### Phase 0 – Product‑Market‑Fit (0‑6 months)  
- Build MVP HEMS gateway (ESP32‑based, CT‑clamps, Wi‑Fi/LTE) + simple app.  
- Run a **10‑home pilot** in Bengaluru/Pune with solar‑rooftop owners; validate bill‑savings (target ≥ 10 %).  
- Secure a **letter of intent** from one state DISCOM for DR participation (IES pilot). 
- Build an emulator to install in the entire sample space from simple homes to CPO (charge point operators)
- Build a rich app experience to show users about what savings potential exists and give actions to suggest HW changes to be done
- Computation done in the cloud using a variety of data points from loads to climate conditions to real time electricity prices
- Use above data to iterate on system sizing to achieve customer KPI changeable via app: efficiency, uptime, savings
- App to allow scaling for automatic bill prediction and show deviations / trend. Gamify the energy consumption trends. Notify customers of good practices by customer and when energy consumption deviates in a negative way which can coach customers about the root cause and potential solutions.

### Phase 1 – Scale Home‑Pilot (6‑18 months)  
- Target early‑adopter urban homeowners via solar‑installer partnerships, online ads, referral bonuses.  
- Reach **5 k homes**; achieve **₹1 Cr ARR**; refine ML models for load forecasting & predictive‑maintenance.  
- File **patents** on India‑specific load‑disaggregation algorithm.  

### Phase 2 – Builder‑B2B Push (12‑30 months)  
- Approach top‑10 residential developers (Prestige, Brigade, DLF, Tata Housing) and PMAY implementing agencies.  
- Offer white‑label kit (gateway + optional 5 kWh battery) pre‑wired in building MEP.  
- Pilot a **500‑unit township**; target GRIHA/LEED credit for “Energy Monitoring & Verification”.  
- Sign **DR aggregation contracts** with state utilities (capacity payment ~₹1‑2 k/kW‑yr).  

### Phase 3 – Geographic Expansion & Data Services (Year 3‑5)  
- Expand to NCR, Hyderabad, Chennai via builder networks.  
- Launch **HEMS‑as‑a‑Service platform** (multitenant cloud) for third‑party OEMs (EV chargers, home batteries).  
- Monetise anonymised load data for grid forecasting, renewable‑integration studies, and carbon‑credit verification.

## 5. Risks & Mitigations  

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|------------|
| **Slow adoption due to high upfront cost** | Medium | High | Offer **zero‑Capex** models (utility or builder‑subsidised hardware); focus on SaaS revenue. |
| **Data privacy / cyber‑security concerns** | Low‑Medium | High | Deploy **end‑to‑end encryption**, local data‑storage option, comply with India’s Personal Data Protection Bill (draft). |
| **Utility DR program delays** | Medium | Medium | Diversify revenue: focus on **bill‑savings** and **ESG compliance** for builders; DR as upside. |
| **Competition from large OEMs (Schneider, Siemens, Samsung)** | Medium | Medium | Differentiate via **India‑specific load‑disaggregation AI**, ultra‑low‑cost hardware, and **builder‑channel exclusivity**. |
| **Regulatory changes (e.g., withdrawal of subsidies)** | Low | Medium | Build business on **core value‑proposition** (energy savings, asset‑life extension) that remains valuable even without subsidies. |

## 6. Recommendation  

1. **Start with a focused home‑pilot** to de‑risk the software, collect labelled load data, and secure early SaaS revenue while building a trusted consumer brand.  
2. **In parallel, engage a marquee residential developer** for a pilot building‑scale deployment; this validates the B2B model, yields quicker volume, and creates a reference project for GRIHA/LEED credits.  
3. **Use home‑pilot data to refine the AI models** that will later be embedded in the builder‑solution, creating a virtuous loop of improvement.  
4. **Target a Series A raise of ₹18‑22 Cr** after 18 months to fund the builder‑scale rollout, expand the data‑science team, and secure utility DR contracts.  

By pursuing **both tracks**, the company captures the **immediate, high‑margin SaaS revenue** from homeowners while simultaneously locking in the **long‑term, volume‑driven B2B opportunity** with residential builders—positioning the venture as the **premier Indian provider of intelligent, behind‑the‑meter energy management** that can later expand into EV‑charging, battery‑storage, and micro‑grid markets.
